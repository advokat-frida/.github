# Privacy should survive Tuesday.

**Advokat Frida makes practical privacy and AI-governance tools for the people who have to turn policies into a working system.** Lawyers, engineers, product teams, operations. Same meeting, fewer
translations.

Small tools. Plain language. Explicit limits. Very little compliance incense.

## Start with something useful

| Project | What it gives you | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| [The Toolkit](https://github.com/advokat-frida/af-toolkit) | Five privacy tools in one browser-only shell, and nothing you type leaves the browser: **SafeSeed** makes test data that is fake by design and proves it with a receipt; **SafeList** checks a send list against the opt-outs and records every decision; **Redactorium** anonymizes, hashes, or redacts a file and hashes the result; **Objection Oracle** rules on whether a review objection blocks a release; **Privacy Wizards Council** gives cited determinations on the questions that eat your afternoon. [Use it.](https://toolkit.advokatfrida.com/) | MIT |
| [IT DEPENDS](https://github.com/advokat-frida/it-depends) | A shared-screen privacy teaching game where one missing detail can change the call. [Play it.](https://advokat-frida.github.io/it-depends/) | Source-visible; reuse is not licensed |

The earlier one-tool repositories (SafeSeed, Build-A-Prompt, Privacy Wizards Council, Objection Oracle) are archived; their code lives on in the Toolkit, one folder per tool, with its history from the day they merged.
