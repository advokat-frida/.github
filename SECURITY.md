# Security policy

Advokat Frida publishes privacy and AI-governance tools. If one of them has a security problem, a
public issue is a poor place to rehearse the exploit.

## Report a vulnerability privately

Use GitHub's **Report a vulnerability** option on the affected repository when it is available.
Otherwise, email [hello@advokatfrida.com](mailto:hello@advokatfrida.com?subject=Private%20security%20report).

Please include:

- the affected repository, version, release, or commit;
- what an attacker could do and who or what could be affected;
- the shortest reliable reproduction steps;
- any relevant browser, runtime, or deployment details; and
- a suggested fix, if you have one.

Do not include real personal data, live credentials, access tokens, or other people's secrets in the
report. Redact them or use a synthetic example. Please do not open a public issue until the report
has been triaged and disclosure has been coordinated.

## Scope and support

Security reports are welcome for the current default branch and current releases of public Advokat
Frida projects. Archived or explicitly parked repositories are not actively supported, though a
report about a material risk in one is still worth sending.

This is a disclosure channel, not a bug-bounty program. We will acknowledge a usable report, assess
its impact, and coordinate next steps without promising a severity label or deadline before the
facts are in.
